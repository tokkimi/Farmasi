## Prompt de composition

Create a refined clean fashion campaign cutout based on this reference's composition. Two clearly adult East Asian models aged 28-32, male at left and female at right, facing one another across a small chrome reflective tabletop, hands gently resting on cheeks, playful friendly smiling expressions, eyes open. Both fully dressed in contemporary high-neck silver metallic jackets with long sleeves. Keep wet black hair styling, silver rings, fashion editorial photography and original left-right two-person arrangement. Natural lifelike skin, soft luminous photography, crisp believable faces. Simplify the scene radically: only the two models, tabletop, one small polished chrome sphere and one minimal white skincare jar. Remove all floating face collages, text, brush strokes, tattoos, tapes, statues, smoke, splashes and surrounding clutter. Keep figures and tabletop fully inside frame with clean margin all around. Wide 1536x1024 composition. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no backdrop at all, cleanly isolated hair and clothes without grey/white fringes; transparent spaces between arms and models. No painted checkerboard. Non-suggestive beauty/skincare fashion photograph.
